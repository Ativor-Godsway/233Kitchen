# +233 Kitchen: your website guide

This guide is for running the kitchen's website day to day. You don't need any technical
knowledge. Everything happens in your **admin area**, on your phone or computer.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to your website and add **/admin** to the end of the address
   (for example `233kitchen.com/admin`). There is also an **Owner login** link at the very bottom
   of every page.
2. Enter your email and password, then tap **Log in**. Tap the **eye** icon to see what you typed.
3. You stay logged in while you use it. After 12 hours without using it, or after a week, you'll be
   asked to log in again. That's normal and keeps your account safe.

On a phone, the main sections are in the bar at the bottom: **Dashboard, Orders, Prep sheet,
Customers**, and **More** for everything else.

---

## 2. New orders

- Every new order is **emailed to you straight away** (subject starts with 🧾 _New order_).
- While the admin is open, a pop-up and a sound tell you about new orders. Tap the bell icon to
  turn the sound on or off.
- **Orders** lists everything. New orders have a red **New** label. Search by name, phone, email or
  order number (e.g. `233-0012`), and filter by pickup date, status or payment.
- Tap an order to open it. You'll see the customer's details (tap the phone number to call or
  text), what they ordered with any notes, their pickup time and how they're collecting.

## 3. Confirming orders and marking them paid

Inside an order:

1. Tap **Confirm order**. The customer gets a "your order is confirmed" email with the payment
   details. You can untick the email or add a personal note before confirming.
2. On the day, tap **Start preparing**, then **Mark ready for pickup** (the customer is emailed),
   then **Mark completed** once it's collected.
3. When the customer pays, tap how they paid under **Payment**: **Zelle**, **Apple Pay** or
   **Cash**. Nothing is paid online, so this is how you keep track.
4. To cancel, tap **Cancel order** (the customer is emailed). Cancelled orders free up their pickup
   time and don't count in your totals.

**Internal notes** in each order are only for you. Customers never see them.

---

## 4. The prep sheet (cooking list)

**Prep sheet** shows everything for one pickup day:

- **Totals**: how many of each dish, and how many of each extra (e.g. 14 × extra plantain), so you
  know exactly what to cook.
- How many orders are in each pickup window, and how many are pickups or Uber couriers.
- **Order slips** for every order, sorted by pickup time.

Tap **Print slips** to print the list and a slip for each box. **Export CSV** downloads it for a
spreadsheet. Choose another day at the top.

---

## 5. Pausing orders and closed weeks

Go to **Settings → Ordering**:

- **Pause ordering**: switch on to stop new orders, for example for a week off. Customers can still
  look at the menu and see your message (**Message shown while paused**). Switch it off to open
  again. Remember to press **Save settings** at the bottom.
- **Closed dates**: add a specific date (like a holiday) when there's no pickup.
- You can also change pickup days, pickup times, how many orders each time slot takes, and when
  orders close (normally Monday 11:59 PM for Wednesday).

---

## 6. Menu: dishes, prices and sold out

Go to **Menu**:

- **Sold out:** use the switch next to a dish. Off shows "Sold out" on the website and nobody can
  order it. You can also switch off a single extra (for example, no more tilapia).
- **Change a price or description:** tap **Edit** on the dish, change it, and press **Save
  changes**. New orders use the new price straight away; orders already placed keep their price.
- **Add a dish:** tap **Add item**.

---

## 7. Marketing emails

Go to **Marketing**:

1. Choose who it goes to, for example **Everyone opted in**. Only customers who ticked "Email me
   about new menu items and offers" ever get marketing emails.
2. Write a subject and your message. You can add a picture link and a button (for example
   "Order now").
3. Tap **Preview** to see the email and how many people will get it, then **Send test to me** to
   check it in your own inbox.
4. Tap **Send** and confirm. **Keep the page open** until it finishes.

Every marketing email has an **Unsubscribe** link, and anyone who unsubscribes is never emailed
again.

**The daily email limit.** Your emails are sent from your Gmail account, and Gmail allows about
**500 emails a day**. To keep room for order emails, marketing emails **stop at 400 a day**. The
Preview tells you if you're close. If a send stops at the limit, it is **paused**, not lost:
the next day, go to **Marketing → Campaign history** and tap **Resume**. Nobody gets it twice.

You can also email one customer directly: open them in **Customers** and tap **Email**. This works
even if they didn't sign up for marketing, so use it only for messages about their order.

---

## 8. Changing your password

1. Go to **Settings** and scroll to **Change password**.
2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice (at least 10 characters). Tap the
   **eye** icon to check what you typed.
3. Tap **Change password**. Any other phone or computer where you were logged in is logged out.

Choose a password you don't use anywhere else. If you forget it, contact us (below) and we'll set a
new one for you.

---

## 9. Checking that emails work

**Settings → Notifications → Send test email to me** sends you a test email straight away. If
something is wrong, it says what. **Email log** lists every email the website has sent; anything
that failed is marked in red and can be sent again with **Resend**.
